Features

Everything an FYP needs

Replace scattered messages, paper forms and loose files with one connected system.

Dashboards for every role

Students, supervisors and the FYP office each see exactly what they need.

Milestones and deadlines

Clear due dates, live progress bars and a calendar that shows what is next.

Documents with versions

Upload PDFs, keep every version and let supervisors preview them in the browser.

Feedback with next steps

Supervisor comments always come with a clear action for the students.

Notifications

Everyone is told when a proposal is decided, a file arrives or a deadline is added.

Accessible by design

Text size, high contrast, readable fonts and read-aloud are one click away.

Who it is for

One portal, three roles

Each account opens a portal designed around its own tasks.

Students

  • Register the project and group
  • Upload proposals and documents
  • Track milestones and read feedback

Supervisors

  • Read the abstract and PDF in one place
  • Approve, request changes or reject
  • Follow progress across all projects

FYP office

  • Manage students and supervisors
  • Assign supervisors within capacity
  • Set deadlines for everyone at once
How it works

From idea to final demo

Four simple steps keep every project moving.

Register

Students register the project, the group and a preferred supervisor.

Get approved

A supervisor is assigned and reviews the proposal document.

Work and submit

Milestones appear. Students upload documents as they go.

Improve

Feedback and next steps guide the project to completion.
